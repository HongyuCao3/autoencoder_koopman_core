\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}
% RQ lead added 2026-09-20. Two top-level questions, one per experiment line, each with named
% sub-questions so that the subsection conclusions can answer them by label. Mapping to the
% Introduction: contribution 1 <-> RQ1a, contribution 2 <-> RQ1b, contribution 3 <-> RQ2.
% Sub-question glosses trimmed 2026-09-21 (experiments_trim_analysis): each subsection's Question restates them.
The experiments answer two questions, one per experiment line.
\textbf{RQ1 (modeling).} Does the learned operator predict a multi-turn attribute from observable readings alone?
Its parts are (1a) history versus the current reading and (1b) the linear case versus a learned lifting or an LSTM (\S\ref{sec:exp_prediction}), and (1c) what the memory state carries (\S\ref{sec:exp_memory}).
\textbf{RQ2 (control).} Does predictive control built on that operator raise constraint satisfaction on a frozen model?
Its parts are (2a) against no control and (2b) against two white-box latent controllers, both in \S\ref{sec:exp_control}.
\S\ref{sec:exp_analysis} is an analysis rather than a further question: it states when a gain on RQ1 can become a gain on RQ2.
