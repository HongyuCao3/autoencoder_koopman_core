\textbf{The Defense column.} Three properties of that column are not visible in Table~\ref{tab:prediction} and apply to every reading of it.  % user ruling 2026-09-22: caveat lives here, not in the caption or the main text

The column rests on two data seeds, and its intervals resample the thirty attacks rather than the seeds.  % n_main_136-151 supersede_reason; MAIN_TABLE_DESIGN.md decision 6
In rollout error the six rows span 0.0686 to 0.0776 around the Stateless null of 0.0736, a range under 13\% of that null.  % docs/experiments/behavioral_surrogate_rows_results.md, absolute table
