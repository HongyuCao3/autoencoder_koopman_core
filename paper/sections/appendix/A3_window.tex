Table~\ref{tab:setup_per_task} lists the per-task settings behind Table~\ref{tab:prediction}.
State depth ranges from 2 to 4 readings and horizon from 3 to 6 steps.
The depth is the deepest state that still leaves a rollout of at least three steps on that task's trajectory length.  % lag/nu rule: docs/experiments/behavioral_surrogate_rows_results.md, core_surrogate_rows_results.md; CORRECTED 2026-09-23 from "1 to 4" (lag counts previous readings, depth = lag+1)
Scored held-out rows per task range from 30 to 12,376, counted after windowing.  % RECOVERED 2026-09-19 (Opus, evidence/numbers.yaml)
The trajectories those rows come from number 10 to 100 for the seven columns of Table~\ref{tab:prediction}, and 4 to 30 for the columns of Appendix~\ref{app:A2_small_columns}.  % group counts in evidence/numbers.yaml `test` fields; CORRECTED 2026-09-23 from "40 to 100" (sentiment has 10, defense 30)
Every interval reported in \S\ref{sec:exp_prediction} is a paired grouped bootstrap over trajectories, with 2000 resamples. 

The two experiment lines report different metrics in different reference frames.  % numbers_benchmark.yaml unit field
The modeling line's skill score is referenced against a best-trivial-null baseline and can go negative, while the control line's percentage carries no reference point.

The matched-window analysis of \S\ref{sec:exp_memory} fits every state depth on the same set of transitions; Table~\ref{tab:matched_window} lists the per-task numbers behind Figure~\ref{fig:memory}.
The window convention matters. On sentence length the memory gain over Last-turn is $+0.603$ under the default window of Table~\ref{tab:prediction} and $+0.109$ under the matched window, so the two conventions answer different questions.  % n_main_013 (default) vs n_abl_127 (matched); moved from Sec. 4.3 on 2026-09-21
The shrinkage appears where the default window lets a shallower state train on more transitions, which is the case on the four core-line tasks and on CEFR.  % core line: lag=3 default drops the first three turns of every trajectory from training; tsar_cefr lag=1
On defense and constraint the default fit is the matched fit, and the two gains coincide.  % behavioral line: window defined by the deepest state, all rows on the same absolute turns (behavioral_surrogate_rows_results.md)
Under either convention the second reading carries most of the gain; the third and fourth readings add $+0.054$ and $+0.027$ on constraint and stay within $\pm 0.004$ elsewhere.  % n_abl_237 (nu3-nu2), n_abl_239 (nu4-nu3); n_abl_126/128/186/188/197/199
